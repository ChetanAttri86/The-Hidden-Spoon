🧂 Discover Salt

Did you know?

Our bodies need sodium, but consuming too much sodium over time can be unhealthy.
🥔 Processed foods such as chips, instant noodles and packaged foods can contain significant amounts of sodium.
🔍 One simple habit is to check the nutrition label before choosing packaged food.

🍭 Discover Sugar

Where can added sugar hide?

Soft drinks, sweets, biscuits, desserts and many packaged foods can contain added sugars.
🥤 Drinks can be an easy way to consume a lot of sugar without feeling very full.
🔍 Reading the nutrition label can help us understand how much sugar a product contains.

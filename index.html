🧂 Discover Salt

What is salt? Salt contains sodium. Our bodies need sodium for important functions, but too much sodium in our diet is not a healthy choice.
🔍 Where can it hide? Packaged snacks, instant noodles, sauces, processed foods and some ready-to-eat meals can contain significant amounts of sodium.
🏷️ Become a label detective When buying packaged food, look at the nutrition label and check the sodium content.
💡 Avyaan's tip Don't judge food only by how it looks or tastes. The nutrition label can tell us much more.

🍭 Discover Sugar

What is added sugar? Some foods naturally contain sugars. Added sugars are sugars added during preparation or processing.
🥤 Watch the drinks Sugary drinks can contain a surprising amount of added sugar and may not make us feel as full as solid food.
🍪 Common places to look Biscuits, sweets, desserts, chocolate, sweetened drinks and many packaged foods may contain added sugars.
🏷️ Read the label Check the serving size and the sugar information on the nutrition label.
